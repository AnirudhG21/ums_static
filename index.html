Services

Comprehensive Water Technology Services

Professional installation, maintenance and support services for domestic and industrial water purification and treatment systems.

Domestic Services

  • Water Purifier Installation

    Professional installation of RO, UV and RO+UV domestic purifiers with proper plumbing and testing.

  • Regular Servicing & Maintenance

    Periodic servicing to ensure peak performance and consistent water quality from your purifier.

  • RO Membrane Replacement

    Timely replacement of RO membranes with genuine parts to restore TDS reduction and water output.

  • Filter Replacement

    Replacement of sediment, carbon and post-filters to maintain optimal purification stages.

  • Troubleshooting & Repair

    Diagnosis and repair of water purifier faults including leaks, low pressure and electrical issues.

  • Spare Parts Replacement

    Supply and replacement of genuine components including pumps, valves and flow restrictors.

Industrial Services

  • Industrial RO System Installation

    Complete installation of industrial RO plants with pre-treatment, control panels and commissioning.

  • RO Plant Maintenance

    Scheduled maintenance contracts for industrial RO plants to ensure continuous, reliable operation.

  • UV System Servicing

    UV lamp replacement, sleeve cleaning and system calibration for industrial UV disinfection units.

  • Water Treatment System Inspection

    Comprehensive inspection and performance assessment of industrial water treatment installations.

  • Spare Parts & Component Support

    Supply of industrial-grade RO membranes, high-pressure pumps, valves and control components.
